SONDER DOCS

Limits are earned back one hand at a time.

Sonder is an Android screen-time app with a twist. You pick the apps you want to limit. When you open one, Sonder steps in front of it and deals you a hand of blackjack:

✓ WIN → BANK + STAKE ▣ LOSS → BANK − STAKE ⌛ BANK 0 → GATE ♠ ONLY TIME USED IS BILLED

What you win is a bank of time that only runs down while the app is actually in front of you — leave, and it waits for you. Bet a 2, 5 or 10 minute chip, or go all in; losing takes the stake off the bank and nothing more, down to zero. Hit zero and the gate is the only way back in.

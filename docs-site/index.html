SONDER DOCS

Limits are earned back one hand at a time.

Sonder is an Android screen-time app with a twist. You pick the apps you want to limit. When you open one, Sonder steps in front of it and deals you a hand of blackjack:

✓ WIN → +5:00 ACCESS ▣ LOSS → +10:00 DEBT ⌛ DEBT CAP → 60:00 ♠ ABSENT 60s → REVOKED

Losing doesn't just cost the hand — it adds lockout debt. You can keep gambling to pay it off, or walk away and serve the wait. Leave a granted app for more than 60 seconds and access is revoked, even with time on the clock.

Data schema

  • TargetEntity — package, label, enabled flag, added-at, maxMillis, blockScope
  • TimeBankEntity — package, remainingMillis, epochDay, lastSeenMillis, emptySinceMillis
  • HandEntity — timestamp, outcome, stakeMillis, bankAfterMillis, the two hands and the label as played (Stats screen)

Three tables, because grants, debt, lockouts and the daily tally were four descriptions of one thing: how much time is left in this app. A bank row carries its own day, so yesterday's remainder is simply not today's access. All times are absolute epoch millis.

Detection and the billing rule

The accessibility service watches window-state changes only (TYPE_WINDOW_STATE_CHANGED), filtered to target packages, self, system dialogs, and notifications. Events are debounced. While a banked target is in front, the coordinator refreshes lastSeenMillis at most every few seconds, and each refresh drains the bank by the elapsed gap — clamped to ten seconds — which is what makes the drain foreground-only by construction. There is no absence rule and nothing revokes a bank (rules → what the clock counts).

Two probes run beside the decision: a media pause when the blocker goes up over a short-form surface, and a sweep for a gated app left floating in picture-in-picture, which is paused and covered the same way.

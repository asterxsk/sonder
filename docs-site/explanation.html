WHY BLACKJACK?

Sonder's premise: the cost of opening a distracting app should be paid in attention, not in money or in a notification you can swipe away.

Friction as a feature

Most screen-time tools rely on willpower at the moment of temptation — the exact moment you have the least of it. Sonder moves the cost earlier: to get in, you must play a hand. Losing doesn't just fail to reward you; it raises the price. The 5-minute access window then forces a deliberate re-entry each time, converting "one quick scroll" into a conscious decision that costs a win per five minutes.

Why debt instead of hard lockout

A hard lockout after a loss is a dead end: it teaches nothing and invites uninstalling. The debt model (v2's design) keeps the door visibly open — every win pays 10 minutes back, so recovery is always 1–6 hands away. This mirrors commitment devices in behavioral economics: punishment that's escapable through effort sustains motivation, while punishment that's escapable only by quitting the tool destroys it. The 60-minute cap exists for the same reason — it bounds the spiral.

Why chance, not a quiz or a hold

  • Quizzes become speed-runnable; you pattern-match the answers in three days.
  • Timed holds are pure dead time; you wait them out on the lock screen and the habit loop stays intact.
  • A hand of chance can't be grooved into muscle memory. Each gate session is genuinely uncertain, so the "maybe I'll just lose and be locked out" calculus stays honest.

Blackjack specifically is fast (~10 seconds per hand), has clean rules that fit a phone screen, and — unlike loot-box mechanics — the stakes are time, never money. That distinction matters for both ethics and Play Store review: nothing here is wagered, purchased, or won of monetary value.

The honesty problem

No enforcement app can survive a determined user — clear data, disable accessibility, uninstall. Sonder doesn't pretend otherwise. The design target is the middle of the night version of you: enough friction that auto-pilot doesn't get through, without requiring heroics from the morning version who has to set it up. Targets are chosen once, on purpose, and the reset procedure is documented (guides → reset) precisely so the honest path is as easy as the dishonest one.

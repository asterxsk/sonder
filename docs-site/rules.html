Enforcement internals Why blackjack?

ACCESS RULES

The complete contract between you and the machine. One number per app — a bank of usable time — and a blackjack hand that moves it. These rules are implemented in pure Kotlin and covered by 109 unit tests.

The time bank

Each limited app has a bank of time measured in milliseconds. The bank is the only thing that decides whether you get in:

  • Bank above zero → the app opens, no questions asked.
  • Bank at zero → the gate, chips greyed, and the morning's stake is what reopens it.

A bank belongs to a local calendar day, and it opens every morning with the 2:00 chip already on it. Nothing else carries over — yesterday's leftovers are not today's access — but a day never opens on nothing, because a table with no chip on it is a table with no way back in.

Hand outcomes

ResultEffect
WinBank + the stake, clamped to the app's maximum
LossBank − the stake, floored at zero — never negative
PushFree replay — no effect at all, hand re-dealt

You choose the stake before the deal: a 2:00, 5:00 or 10:00 chip, or ALL IN — the whole bank. A chip is backed by the bank: you can only bet what you hold, so the 5:00 chip stays greyed out until the bank covers it, and a win is always paid out of time that was at risk. A bank too thin for the smallest chip is a table with nothing to deal at all — chips greyed, DEAL greyed, and the gate waiting for the morning.

Worked example

Bank 2:00 from the morning's stake, you bet the 2:00 chip and win: bank 4:00. Bet ALL IN on 4:00 and win: bank 8:00. Two more all-in wins would read 16:00 and 32:00 — and the ceiling stops it there, whatever the app's maximum is set to. Lose the 4:00 instead and the bank is 0:00: out of time, the chips greyed, and the table opens again at the next morning's stake.

What the clock counts

Time is spent by using the app, never by the wall clock:

  • While a banked app is in the foreground, the bank is drained by the elapsed time — stamped in steps of at most 10 seconds.
  • Time away is not billed. Leave a 29:00 bank and come back an hour later: it still reads 29:00.
  • Nothing revokes a bank. There is no absence threshold and no timer to race; the drain is the only thing that ever takes time out.

The step clamp is also what protects you from the machine: a reboot, a killed process or a stalled service cannot bill a gap you were not present for.

The maximum is a lid, not a floor: a win that would push the bank past it lands on it, and a loss is never clamped up to it.

Draining a bank to zero also locks the limit's removal for 12 hours. It is a padlock on the settings screen, not on the app: the gate behaves exactly as it always does, and winning any hand clears the lock immediately.

Table rules

  • Single 52-card deck, reshuffled before each hand.
  • Dealer stands on all 17s including soft 17.
  • Blackjack on the deal (natural) = instant win, no play needed.
  • HIT and STAND only — no double, split, or surrender. Deliberately austere.
  • After a resolved hand the panel keeps PLAY AGAIN beside CONTINUE, so the bank you just built can be bet again — up to the ceiling, or down to zero.

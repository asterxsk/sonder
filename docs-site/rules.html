Enforcement internals Why blackjack?

ACCESS RULES

The complete contract between you and the machine. One number per app — a bank of usable time — and a blackjack hand that moves it. These rules are implemented in pure Kotlin and covered by 110 unit tests.

The time bank

Each limited app has a bank of time measured in milliseconds. The bank is the only thing that decides whether you get in:

  • Bank above zero → the app opens, no questions asked.
  • Bank at zero → the gate. The app is locked, and the way in is a won hand.

A bank belongs to a local calendar day, and nothing carries over: yesterday's leftover 30:00 is not today's access. An app added a minute ago has banked nothing either. Both read 0, and both open to the gate rather than to the app — the bank is what access is, and nothing grants it but a hand that was won.

Hand outcomes

ResultEffect
WinBank + the stake, clamped to the app's maximum
LossBank − the stake, floored at zero — never negative
PushFree replay — no effect at all, hand re-dealt

You choose the stake before the deal: a 2:00, 5:00 or 10:00 chip, or ALL IN — the whole bank. A chip is backed by the bank: you can only bet what you hold, so the 5:00 chip stays greyed out until the bank covers it, and a win is always paid out of time that was at risk. The smallest chip is the one exception — 2:00 is the table's own stake, and the table deals it for whatever the bank holds, including nothing. It is the seat at the table rather than time you own: it buys no access of its own, and only a hand won with it puts anything in the bank. That is what keeps a locked app one hand away instead of a wall.

Worked example

Bank empty, the table deals the 2:00 chip and you win: bank 2:00. Bet the 2:00 chip and win again: bank 4:00. Bet ALL IN on 4:00 and win: bank 8:00 — two more all-in wins would read 16:00 and 32:00, and the ceiling stops it there, whatever the app's maximum is set to. Lose the 4:00 instead and the bank is 0:00: the gate, every chip above 2:00 greyed, and the table still there to be played for the smallest one.

What the clock counts

Time is spent by using the app, never by the wall clock:

  • While a banked app is in the foreground, the bank is drained by the elapsed time — stamped in steps of at most 10 seconds.
  • Time away is not billed. Leave a 29:00 bank and come back an hour later: it still reads 29:00.
  • Nothing revokes a bank. There is no absence threshold and no timer to race; the drain is the only thing that ever takes time out.

The step clamp is also what protects you from the machine: a reboot, a killed process or a stalled service cannot bill a gap you were not present for.

The maximum is a lid, not a floor: a win that would push the bank past it lands on it, and a loss is never clamped up to it.

Draining a bank to zero also locks the limit's removal for 12 hours. It is a padlock on the settings screen, not on the app: the gate behaves exactly as it always does, and winning any hand clears the lock immediately.

Table rules

  • Single 52-card deck, reshuffled before each hand.
  • Dealer stands on all 17s including soft 17.
  • Blackjack on the deal (natural) = instant win, no play needed.
  • HIT and STAND only — no double, split, or surrender. Deliberately austere.
  • After a resolved hand the panel keeps PLAY AGAIN beside CONTINUE, so the bank you just built can be bet again — up to the ceiling, or down to zero.

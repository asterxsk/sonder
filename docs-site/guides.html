Verify enforcement on-device

Nothing is shortened for debug builds any more — there is no debug constant to wait out, because the only clock that matters is the one that runs while you are looking at the app. The constants to know:

ConstantValue
Chips2:00 / 5:00 / 10:00, or ALL IN
Billing step≤ 10 s of foreground time
Maximum bankyour setting, default 60:00
Removal lock after a drained bank12 h
 
# on a target app with an empty bank:
# 1. open it → the gate, with the chips on it
# 2. bet the 2:00 chip and win → you're in, no second screen
# 3. sit in the app for a minute → Home shows the bank about a minute lighter
# 4. leave for two minutes, come back → the bank is where you left it
# 5. bet ALL IN and lose → bank 0, Home reads LOCKED, REMOVE LIMIT is locked
# 6. reopen the target → the gate is back, playable from nothing

Watch the state badges on Home: ✓ GRANTED ▣ LOCKED. The gate itself carries the other two: ♠ PLAYING ⌛ OUT OF TIME.

Add or remove targets

  1. Open TARGETS from the dock and tap ADD.
  2. Search the picker and check the apps you want limited.
  3. Rows in the list are inert. The › arrow opens the app's own settings, where the maximum and the scope are set, and where REMOVE LIMIT lives — behind a 30-second hold.

Sonder itself

Sonder excludes itself from the picker — you can't gate the gatekeeper. The system launcher and settings are also ignored by the detector so you can always reach the controls.

Grow the bank, or spend it

  • Small chips: 2:00 and 5:00 bets make a loss cheap — the way to feel out a bad streak without handing back the evening.
  • ALL IN: the whole bank on one hand. Doubles it on a win, ends it on a loss.
  • Play again: after a win the panel keeps PLAY AGAIN beside CONTINUE, so the bank you just built can be bet again — up to the ceiling you set.
  • Push: nothing moves and the hand is re-dealt. Free.

Exact arithmetic: Access rules → hand outcomes.

Survive OEM battery managers

Xiaomi, Samsung, Huawei, and friends suspend background services. If the gate stops appearing after a day or two:

  1. Exclude Sonder from battery optimization (Settings → Apps → Sonder → Battery → Unrestricted).
  2. Disable "battery optimization" for the accessibility service if your OEM splits it out.
  3. Lock Sonder in the recents screen where supported.
  4. After any Sonder update, re-check accessibility — Android may have dropped it.

Reset Sonder state

Banks, targets, and hand history live in a local Room database. To start over: Android Settings → Apps → Sonder → Storage → Clear data. That's also the honest way to cheat — which is why targets are chosen once and meant to be kept.
